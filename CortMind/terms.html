3. Description of Service

CoRT Circle is a digital thinking tool that implements the CoRT (Cognitive Research Trust) methodology developed by Dr. Edward de Bono. The App provides six structured thinking frameworks to help users organize their thoughts, make better decisions, and solve problems systematically:

  • PMI (Plus, Minus, Interesting)
  • CAF (Consider All Factors)
  • C&S (Consequences & Sequel)
  • AGO (Aims, Goals, Objectives)
  • APC (Alternatives, Possibilities, Choices)
  • Action Planning

The App supports handwritten note-taking via Apple Pencil and offers three optional AI modes — AI Coach (a conversational coach that can propose and, with your confirmation, execute in-app actions such as creating sessions, filling tools, running chains and exporting), Thinking Chain (a guided six-tool analysis) and PI Diverge (lateral-thinking expansion) — plus per-step AI analysis, polish and scoring. AI features require a separate API key from the provider you configure.
