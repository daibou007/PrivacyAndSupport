How to Use TelePrompt Live

1. Writing a Script

Tap the + button on the Scripts screen to create a new script. Type or paste your text into the editor, then tap Done to save it.

2. Playing the Teleprompter

Play - Tap Play in the editor to open the prompter. The first time you play a script, a quick guide explains the on-screen gestures; after that it opens straight into the prompter.
Gestures - Tap anywhere to play or pause, swipe up or down to adjust the reading position, use the bottom controls to change scroll speed, and pinch to resize the text.

3. Mirror Mode (Pro)

Mirror Mode flips the text horizontally and vertically for use with professional beam-splitter teleprompter rigs.

4. Remote Control (Pro)

Open Remote Control in Settings to pair nearby iPhones, iPads, or Macs over the local network. Once paired, you can start, stop, and adjust speed from the paired device without touching the device that is recording. Apple Watch can also be used as a remote, but only when TelePrompt Live is running on iPhone — Apple Watch pairs directly with its companion iPhone and has no iPad or Mac counterpart.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TelePrompt Live?

TelePrompt Live is a teleprompter app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch that helps you read scripts naturally while looking into the camera.

Does TelePrompt Live need an internet connection?

No. Writing and playing scripts works fully offline. A Wi-Fi connection is only needed for local-network Remote Control pairing.

Does TelePrompt Live use voice or speech recognition to scroll?

No. Voice-tracking auto-scroll has been removed from the app. Scrolling is controlled manually by speed setting or gestures, and the app does not request microphone access.

Where are my scripts stored?

Scripts are stored locally on your device only. We do not store your scripts on our own servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I unlock Pro features?

Mirror Mode, Remote Control, and unlimited scripts are available with TelePrompt Live Pro, offered as a monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, processed securely through Apple's App Store.

Can I use my Apple Watch to control TelePrompt Live on iPad or Mac?

No. Apple Watch pairs only with its companion iPhone, so Watch remote control is available exclusively when TelePrompt Live is running on iPhone. On iPad and Mac, use another iPhone, iPad, or Mac as a Remote Control device instead.

The app isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If the issue persists, email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem and your device model and OS version.
