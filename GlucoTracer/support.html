How to Use Gluco Tracer

🩸 1. Recording Blood Glucose

Step 1 — Open the app and tap the + button on the home screen to add a new blood glucose reading.
Step 2 — Enter your blood glucose value in mg/dL (range: 20–600). The app supports the US standard unit only.
Step 3 — Select the detection period: Fasting, Pre-Breakfast, Post-Breakfast, Pre-Lunch, Post-Lunch, Pre-Dinner, Post-Dinner, Bedtime, or Random.
Step 4 — Add optional notes and save. Readings below 70 mg/dL are automatically tagged as hypoglycemia, and readings above 180 mg/dL are tagged as hyperglycemia.
💡 Tip: Consistent tracking across detection periods helps you spot trends over time.

💉 2. Insulin Management

Gluco Tracer includes a built-in insulin brand library with common US brands:

  • Rapid-Acting: Humalog, Novolog, Apidra
  • Short-Acting: Regular (Humulin R, Novolin R)
  • Intermediate-Acting: NPH (Humulin N, Novolin N)
  • Long-Acting: Lantus, Levemir, Tresiba, Toujeo

You can log each injection with dosage, insulin type, injection site (Abdomen, Thigh, Upper Arm), and time. Set up multi-plan daily reminders to stay on schedule.

💊 3. Medication Management

Track your oral diabetes medications with the preset US drug library:

  • Metformin, Glipizide, Sitagliptin, Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin, and more

Each medication entry supports three states:

  • Completed — taken as scheduled
  • Missed — dose was skipped
  • Postponed — taken later than scheduled

Your medication adherence rate is calculated automatically: Completed ÷ Total Planned (shown to 2 decimal places). The app also detects potential over-dose risks.

📊 4. Data Statistics & Analysis

Gluco Tracer aggregates data across all three modules to give you a clear picture:

  • Blood Glucose Compliance Rate — percentage of readings within target range
  • Medication Adherence Rate — how consistently you follow your medication plan
  • Average Daily Insulin Dosage — track your insulin usage over time
  • Abnormal Event Counts — hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia episodes

Switch between daily, weekly, and monthly views. Trend charts help you visualize patterns.

📄 5. PDF Report Generation

Generate comprehensive PDF reports entirely on your device:

  1. Go to the Statistics tab and tap Generate Report.
  2. Select the date range you want to include.
  3. The report includes all blood glucose records, insulin logs, and medication history, sorted chronologically.
  4. Abnormal readings and missed doses are specially marked for easy review.
  5. Share the PDF via the iOS share panel — Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or save to Files.
🔒 Privacy: PDF reports are rendered 100% locally on your device. No data is ever uploaded to a server.

🎨 6. Appearance Settings

Gluco Tracer supports three display modes:

  • Follow System — matches your iPhone's Light/Dark setting automatically
  • Always Light — use the light theme at all times
  • Always Dark — use the dark theme at all times

All charts, cards, and UI elements adapt dynamically to the selected mode. Change this anytime in Settings → Appearance.

⚙️ 7. Customizing Settings

  • Appearance — Choose Dark Mode, Light Mode, or Follow System
  • Notifications — Enable/disable medication and insulin reminders globally
  • Data Overview — View a summary of all stored records
  • Reset All Data — Clear all records and preferences (requires secondary confirmation)
  • Restore Purchase — Restore your one-time in-app purchase on a new device

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Gluco Tracer require an internet connection?

No. Gluco Tracer works completely offline. All calculations, chart rendering, statistics, and PDF generation happen locally on your device. No internet connection is needed for any feature.

Does Gluco Tracer require me to create an account?

No. There is no account system, no registration, and no login. All features are available immediately after installation. Your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Is my health data shared with anyone?

No. All data is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage. Gluco Tracer does not use any cloud services, does not upload any data, and does not share information with any third party. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What blood glucose unit does the app use?

Gluco Tracer uses mg/dL only, which is the standard unit in the United States. The supported range is 20–600 mg/dL.

Is Gluco Tracer a medical device?

No. Gluco Tracer is a general wellness tool for recording and tracking blood glucose, insulin, and medication data. It does not provide medical diagnosis, disease judgment, or treatment recommendations. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical advice.

What insulin brands are supported?

The app includes a built-in library of common US insulin brands: Humalog, Novolog, Apidra (Rapid-Acting); Humulin R, Novolin R (Short-Acting); Humulin N, Novolin N (Intermediate-Acting); Lantus, Levemir, Tresiba, Toujeo (Long-Acting).

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchase. The app uses Apple's official restorePurchases API to verify and restore your one-time in-app purchase.

Where is my data stored?

All blood glucose records, insulin logs, medication records, PDF reports, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Reset All Data. A secondary confirmation dialog will appear to prevent accidental deletion. This action is irreversible.

Does the app support multiple languages?

Gluco Tracer is localized for the United States only and supports American English. Date format is MM/DD/YYYY and time format is 12-hour (hh:mm a).

The app crashed or isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem and your device model & iOS version.
