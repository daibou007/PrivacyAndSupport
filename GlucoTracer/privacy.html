Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Summary: Gluco Tracer is a fully offline, privacy-first blood glucose management app. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All health-related data stays exclusively on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Gluco Tracer ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. Gluco Tracer is designed to work entirely on your device — no account required, no cloud servers, no tracking of any kind.

By using Gluco Tracer, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Gluco Tracer does not collect any personal or health information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or track your usage behavior.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not upload your blood glucose readings, insulin records, medication logs, or any health data to any server.
  • We do not use any third-party SDKs of any kind.

All blood glucose records, insulin logs, medication schedules, app preferences, and generated PDF reports are stored locally on your device using iOS system storage and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. Local Notifications

Gluco Tracer uses iOS local notifications (UNUserNotificationCenter) to provide medication and insulin reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device — no push notification server is involved. You can manage or disable all notifications at any time in the App's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

4. In-App Purchase

Gluco Tracer offers a single one-time in-app purchase to unlock all features. The purchase is processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information — all payment processing is handled exclusively by Apple. Purchase state is cached locally on your device after verification.

5. Data Storage

All data generated by the App (blood glucose records, insulin logs, medication records, statistical data, PDF reports, and app preferences) is stored exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored data. You can also use the "Reset All Data" option in Settings to clear all records at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

Gluco Tracer does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, social login, or data-sharing services. No data is shared with any third party.

7. Children's Privacy

Gluco Tracer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for all ages.

8. FDA General Wellness Notice

Gluco Tracer is a general wellness tool for recording and tracking blood glucose, insulin, and medication data. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, disease judgment, or treatment recommendations. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical advice.

9. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. All your data lives on your device and can be removed simply by uninstalling the App or using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings.
