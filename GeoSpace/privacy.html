Privacy Policy — GeoSpace

GeoSpace is an offline 3D analytic and solid geometry visualization tool for iPad. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect personal data.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

The short version. Every solid you build, every equation you parse and every distance or angle GeoSpace computes is produced on your device. Nothing about you or your work is transmitted to us, because there is nothing in the app that transmits it. GeoSpace has no network feature of any kind.

1. Who we are

GeoSpace is published by Daibou007 Team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It applies to the GeoSpace application and nothing else.

2. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically, GeoSpace does not:

  • create or require an account, a sign-in, or a profile;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
  • collect location data of any kind;
  • collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics;
  • contain advertising, marketing pop-ups, or third-party tracking SDKs;
  • use cookies or any equivalent tracking technology.

3. Information stored on your device

GeoSpace saves your work locally so the app is useful between sessions. This data stays in the app's own storage on your device and is never uploaded:

  • Saved scenes — the solids, equation surfaces, auxiliary elements, measurement annotations, camera state and projection mode of every scene you save from the workspace. Stored as JSON files in the app's Documents folder.
  • What you type — equation text while you are working, and problem text you paste into the Problem Parser; it stays in memory while you edit and is only stored if you save the scene.
  • Preferences — your appearance choice (light, dark or auto), 3D scene rendering options (edge thickness, surface transparency, label size, field of view, camera damping, axes, grid, anti-aliasing), and which guidance tours you have already seen.

You can delete any of this at any time from within the app: saved scenes can be deleted individually or cleared entirely from Settings → Local Data (each deletion is confirmed first), and deleting the app removes all of it.

4. No network features

GeoSpace contains no networking code for its features. Equation parsing, 3D rendering, auxiliary geometry generation, measurement and the problem parser all run entirely offline. The app does not load remote content; the only external links are the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages you are reading, which open in your browser when you choose to open them, and the App Store rating page, which iOS opens when you tap "Rate GeoSpace".

5. Children's privacy

GeoSpace collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, no verifiable parental consent is required under COPPA. The app presents no advertising and contains no social features or user-generated content sharing.

6. Your rights under the CCPA and similar laws

The California Consumer Privacy Act, the GDPR and comparable regimes grant rights of access, deletion, correction and opt-out of the sale of personal information. Because GeoSpace collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, delete, correct or sell. We do not sell or share personal information, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

7. Data security

Your saved scenes and preferences are protected by the app sandbox and, where you have enabled it, by your device passcode and iOS data protection. Because nothing leaves the device, no server-side security measures are involved.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be described in the app's App Store release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: open a support request.
