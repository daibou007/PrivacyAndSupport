Privacy Policy — LinearAlgebraViz

LinearAlgebraViz is an offline linear algebra tool for iPad. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect personal data.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version. Everything LinearAlgebraViz computes is computed on your device. Nothing about you or your work is transmitted to us, because there is nothing in the app that transmits it — the app performs no network requests at all. There are no optional cloud features to configure and no exceptions to this policy.

1. Who we are

LinearAlgebraViz is published by Daibou007 Team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It applies to the LinearAlgebraViz application and nothing else.

2. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically, LinearAlgebraViz does not:

  • create or require an account, a sign-in, or a profile;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
  • collect location data of any kind;
  • collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics;
  • contain advertising, marketing pop-ups, or third-party tracking SDKs;
  • use cookies or any equivalent tracking technology;
  • make any network request — there is no connection to make.

3. Information stored on your device

LinearAlgebraViz saves your work locally so the app is useful between sessions. This data stays in the app's own storage on your device and is never uploaded:

  • Saved scenes — the vectors, matrices, constants and animation state you save from the module pages, written as .lmscene files in the app's Documents folder. Capped only by your device's free space; the Settings page shows the total size.
  • Preferences — your appearance choice, readout precision, animation speed, gesture damping, grid density and label switches.
  • Guide flags — which tutorials you have seen, so the welcome guide and the coach-mark walkthroughs appear once and can be replayed on demand.

You can delete scenes at any time from within the app: each deletion is confirmed first, and Settings → System → "Delete All Scenes" removes every .lmscene file at once. Deleting the app removes all of it. Scenes you export leave your device only through the iOS share sheet, at your own direction.

4. Children's privacy

LinearAlgebraViz collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, no verifiable parental consent is required under COPPA. The app presents no advertising and contains no social features or user-generated content sharing.

5. Your rights under the CCPA and similar laws

The California Consumer Privacy Act, the GDPR and comparable regimes grant rights of access, deletion, correction and opt-out of the sale of personal information. Because LinearAlgebraViz collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, delete, correct or sell. We do not sell or share personal information, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

6. Data security

Your work is protected by the app sandbox and, where you have enabled it, by your device passcode and iOS data protection. Because nothing leaves the device, there is no server-side storage to secure.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be described in the app's App Store release notes.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: open a support request.
