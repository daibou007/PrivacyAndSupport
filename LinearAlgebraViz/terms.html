4. Accuracy and limitations

LinearAlgebraViz computes determinants, ranks, inverses, decompositions, eigenpairs and solution sets using the algorithms described in its built-in user manual. Those algorithms have limits, which the app states where they apply:

  • All arithmetic runs in double precision with partial pivoting, and zero tests use a tolerance of 10⁻⁹. A determinant of 10⁻¹⁷ is reported as zero — by design, not by accident.
  • Symmetric matrices take the Jacobi rotation route to their eigen decomposition; general matrices use the characteristic polynomial (Faddeev–LeVerrier) and a root iteration (Durand–Kerner). The root iteration converges to machine accuracy for the small matrices a course uses, but it is a numerical method, not an exact one.
  • QR and the SVD verify their own reconstruction (‖A − QR‖², ‖A − UΣVᵀ‖²) and report the measured residual, so a factorisation that did not land is visible rather than silently accepted.

Verify important results independently. LinearAlgebraViz is provided as an educational aid. It is not a certified engineering, financial, medical or safety-critical calculation tool, and it must not be relied upon as one.
