Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22 · Applies to: WindFix (iOS)

Short version

WindFix is fully offline. Your audio never leaves your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any data.

Data collection

WindFix collects no data of any kind:

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No network permission — the app cannot send data anywhere
  • No advertising identifiers

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording. Recordings are stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device and are never uploaded. All processing — detection, repair and export — also happens entirely on your device.

Imported audio

Audio you import from the Files app or the share sheet is copied into the app's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Purchases

WindFix is a paid app with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple on the App Store; the app never sees your payment information and contains no purchase-related code.

Deleting your data

Deleting a recording inside the app permanently removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.
