3. What the app does

ComplexEye computes complex functions, renders domain colourings, maps grids under conformal transformations, builds Riemann surfaces, locates and classifies singular points, verifies the Cauchy–Riemann equations numerically, derives complex derivatives symbolically, and iterates fractal systems. All of this computation happens on your device, using the app's own engine.
