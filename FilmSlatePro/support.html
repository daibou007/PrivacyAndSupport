How to Use FilmSlate Pro

🎬 1. Choosing a Slate Mode

FilmSlate Pro offers two input modes to suit your workflow:

Text Mode — Enter production details using text fields in a classic clapperboard layout. Ideal for quick setup and precise metadata entry.
Handwriting Mode — Write directly on the slate with Apple Pencil or your finger using PencilKit. Perfect for mimicking a traditional physical slate on set.

Switch between modes anytime in Settings → Slate Mode.

🎞️ 2. Setting Up Your Slate

Fill in the production details displayed on the digital slate:

  • PROD — Production name (e.g., "The Midnight Shoot")
  • SCENE — Scene number or identifier
  • SHOT — Shot number within the scene
  • TAKE — Take number (auto-increments with each clap)
  • DIR — Director name
  • CAM — Camera or cinematographer identifier

Tap Edit Slate to modify all fields at once. The take number increments automatically each time you clap.

⏱️ 3. Timecode and Frame Rate

The slate displays a real-time timecode readout. Configure your frame rate in Settings → Timecode:

  • 23.976 / 24 fps — Cinema and film production
  • 25 / 50 fps — PAL broadcast standards
  • 29.97 / 30 / 60 fps — NTSC broadcast standards

🔊 4. Recording a Clap

The CLAP button is the core action of FilmSlate Pro:

  1. Set up your production details on the slate.
  2. Tap the CLAP button to simulate a slate clap.
  3. The app plays a clap sound, flashes the screen, and provides haptic feedback.
  4. A shot log entry is automatically created with the current timecode, scene, take, and all production metadata.
  5. The take number automatically increments for the next shot.
💡 Tip: Use the CLAP button at the start of each take, just like a physical slate. The timecode stamp helps sync audio and video in post-production.

📋 5. Viewing Shot History

All your clap records are automatically saved in the shot history:

  1. Tap the History tab at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Browse all recorded shots, sorted by most recent first.
  3. Tap any entry to view full details (timecode, scene, take, production, director, camera).
  4. Use the search bar to filter by scene, production name, or notes.
  5. Swipe left or long-press to delete individual entries.

📤 6. Exporting Shot Logs

Export your shot history as a CSV file for use in editing software, script supervision, or production reports:

  1. Open the History tab.
  2. Tap the Export button in the top-right corner.
  3. Share the CSV file via AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or save to Files.

The CSV includes date, time, production, scene, shot, take, director, camera, timecode, and notes for every recorded shot.

✏️ 7. Using Handwriting Mode

Handwriting Mode lets you write directly on the slate with Apple Pencil or your finger:

  1. Switch to Handwriting Mode in Settings → Slate Mode.
  2. Optionally set a custom background image by tapping Edit Slate.
  3. Write or draw directly on the slate canvas.
  4. The timecode overlay remains visible at the bottom.
  5. Your drawings are saved automatically and persist between sessions.
💡 Tip: Use Handwriting Mode with a custom background image (like a logo or set photo) to create a personalized slate that matches your production's look.

⚙️ 8. Customizing Settings

  • Slate Mode — Switch between Text Mode and Handwriting Mode
  • Timecode Frame Rate — Set your preferred frame rate (23.976–60 fps)
  • Dark Mode — Toggle dark mode on/off (recommended for on-set use to reduce screen glare)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FilmSlate Pro?

FilmSlate Pro is a digital clapperboard (film slate) app designed for film, television, and video production. It replaces a traditional physical slate with a feature-rich digital alternative that records shot metadata and exports production logs.

Does FilmSlate Pro require an internet connection?

No. FilmSlate Pro works completely offline. All features — slate display, clap recording, shot logging, and CSV export — work without an internet connection. This makes it ideal for on-set use where connectivity may be limited.

Does FilmSlate Pro require any device permissions?

No. FilmSlate Pro does not request access to your photo library, microphone, camera, contacts, or any other sensitive device resource. It's a self-contained app that needs no special permissions.

What devices does FilmSlate Pro support?

FilmSlate Pro is designed for iPad and requires iPadOS 16.0 or later. The larger screen makes it ideal for on-set use as a digital slate.

Where is my shot log data stored?

All shot logs, production metadata, drawings, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS system storage (Core Data and UserDefaults). Nothing is transmitted to external servers. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Can I export my shot logs for use in post-production?

Yes. You can export all shot logs as a CSV file from the History tab. The CSV includes date, time, timecode, scene, take, production, director, camera, and notes — making it easy to import into editing software or production reports.

Does FilmSlate Pro collect any personal data?

No. FilmSlate Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. All data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I reset the take number?

Tap Edit Slate from the main screen, then manually set the take number to your desired value. The take counter will increment from that point onward with each clap.

The app crashed or isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem and your device model & iPadOS version.
