Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Summary: FilmSlate Pro is a fully offline, privacy-first digital film slate app. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All shot logs, preferences, and data stay entirely on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how FilmSlate Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. FilmSlate Pro is a digital clapperboard designed for film production — it works entirely on your device with no account, no server, and no tracking.

By using FilmSlate Pro, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

FilmSlate Pro does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or track your usage behavior.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not upload your production data, shot logs, or any content to any server.

All production information (production name, scene, shot, take, director, camera), shot logs, timecode data, handwriting drawings, background images, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS system storage (UserDefaults and Core Data) and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. No Permissions Required

FilmSlate Pro does not request access to your photo library, microphone, camera, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device resource. The app functions completely offline and requires no special permissions to operate.

4. Data Storage

All data generated by the App (shot logs, production metadata, handwriting drawings, custom background images, app preferences) is stored exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

FilmSlate Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social login services. No data is shared with any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

FilmSlate Pro does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and is safe for all ages.

7. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. All your data lives on your device and can be removed simply by uninstalling the App.

If you have questions or concerns, please contact us at daibou007@gmail.com.
