How to Use Beat Forger

🎵 1. Basic Metronome Controls

Step 1 — Tap the large play button to start the metronome. The pulsing display shows the current beat in real time.
Step 2 — Adjust BPM using the slider or tap the tempo display to use Tap Tempo (tap at least 3 times for accurate detection).
Step 3 — Change the time signature by tapping the signature display. Choose from presets like 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, and more.
💡 Tip: The metronome supports 1–300 BPM with 1 BPM precision for professional accuracy.

🎼 2. Subdivisions & Accent Patterns

Subdivisions — Choose from quarter notes, eighths, sixteenths, triplets, quintuplets, sextuplets, and septuplets for complex rhythmic patterns.
Accent Patterns — Customize accent levels for each beat: ghost notes, normal beats, accented beats, and rim shots. Create polyrhythmic patterns with independent voices.
Sound Kits — Select from 6 sound kits: Classic Wood, Electronic, Drum Kit, Cowbell, Rimshot, and Hi-Hat.

📝 3. Song Structure Builder

Step 1 — Go to the Songs tab and create a new song. Add sections (e.g., Intro, Verse, Chorus) with individual settings.
Step 2 — Configure each section with its own tempo, time signature, bar count, and accent pattern.
Step 3 — Add tempo ramps within sections for gradual tempo changes with curve options (linear, ease-in, ease-out).
Step 4 — Choose a play mode: Full Song (play through once), A-B-A Loop, or Practice Loop (repeat last N bars).

⏱ 4. Practice Timer & History

Step 1 — Go to the Practice tab and set your practice duration.
Step 2 — Start the timer and practice along with the metronome. The app tracks your session automatically.
Step 3 — View your practice history in the calendar view, with color-coded intensity indicators based on practice duration.
Auto Tempo Ramp — Enable automatic tempo ramping to gradually increase BPM during your practice session.

🚀 5. Speed Drill Mode

Step 1 — Go to the Speed Drill tab to set up progressive tempo training.
Step 2 — Configure start BPM, end BPM, step size, and bars per step.
Step 3 — The ladder visualization shows your progress. The metronome automatically advances to the next tempo when you maintain the target.

⚙️ 6. Settings & Appearance

In Settings, you can customize:

  • Sound Kit — Choose your preferred click sound.
  • Theme — Switch between Dark, Stage Red, and High Contrast themes.
  • Haptic Feedback — Toggle vibration feedback on supported devices.
  • Practice Timer — Set default practice duration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Beat Forger collect any personal data?

No. Beat Forger does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data is stored locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use Beat Forger offline?

Yes. Beat Forger works completely offline. All audio playback, practice tracking, and song features function without an internet connection.

What is the BPM range?

Beat Forger supports 1–300 BPM with 1 BPM precision, suitable for any musical genre or practice level.

How do Speed Drills work?

Speed Drill mode helps you progressively build speed. Set a start and end BPM, and the app automatically increases tempo at your configured interval. A visual ladder tracks your progress through each tempo step.

Where is my practice data stored?

All practice sessions, songs, and settings are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. Nothing is sent to external servers. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Is Beat Forger a one-time purchase?

Yes. Beat Forger is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or recurring charges.

The app crashed or isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem, your device model, and iOS version.
