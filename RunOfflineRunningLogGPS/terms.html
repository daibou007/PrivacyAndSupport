Heart-rate limit and heart-rate drift ("heat stress") alerts are heuristic coaching hints computed from heart rate alone, after the condition has been sustained for a period. They can be delayed, missed or wrong — for example if the watch is loose, if your heart rate rises very quickly, or if notifications are silenced. They are not a substitute for how you feel and must never be relied on to prevent injury or illness.

Consult a physician before beginning or changing a training programme. Stop and seek help if you feel unwell — chest pain, dizziness, faintness or unusual breathlessness. When running outdoors, watch the road, obey traffic signals and local law, and do not stare at the screen. Use the voice announcements or haptics instead of reading the display.
