Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Summary: Melon Pitcher is a fully offline, privacy-first watermelon ripeness detection app. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All audio processing happens entirely on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Melon Pitcher ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. Melon Pitcher is designed to work entirely on your device - no account, no server, no tracking.

By using Melon Pitcher, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Melon Pitcher does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or track your usage behavior.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not upload your audio data, detection history, or any content to any server.

All detection history (watermelon variety, ripeness level, pitch frequency, Brix value) and app preferences are stored locally on your device using SQLite and UserDefaults, and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. Microphone Permission

Melon Pitcher requests access to your device microphone to capture and analyze the pitch frequency of knocking sounds on a watermelon - this is the core functionality of the app. The microphone is only active during an active detection session and stops automatically once three valid knocks are captured. All audio captured by the microphone is processed locally on your device using on-device signal processing (FFT-based pitch analysis).

Raw audio data is automatically and permanently deleted within 2 seconds after the pitch analysis is complete. No audio recordings are ever saved to your device or uploaded to any server.

You can revoke microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

4. Detection History

Melon Pitcher stores a local history of your past detections (watermelon variety, ripeness level, pitch frequency, and Brix sweetness estimate) so you can review them later. All detection records are stored exclusively on your device using SQLite. You can delete individual records or clear all local data at any time from the History or Settings page within the app.

5. Data Storage

All data generated by the App (detection history and app preferences) is stored exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the App, or using the "Clear All Local Data" option in Settings, will permanently delete all locally stored data.

6. Third-Party Services

Melon Pitcher does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social login services. No data is shared with any third party. Network access is only used to open the App Store page for sharing and rating the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Melon Pitcher does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for use by all age groups.

8. CCPA Compliance (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected - None is collected.
  • Delete personal information - All data is stored locally and can be deleted from within the app at any time.
  • Opt-out of sale of personal information - We do not sell any data as we collect none.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights.

Because Melon Pitcher collects zero personal data, all CCPA rights are inherently satisfied.

9. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. All your data lives on your device and can be removed simply by uninstalling the App or using the "Clear All Local Data" option in Settings.
