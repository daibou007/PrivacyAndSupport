How to Use Melon Pitcher

1. Getting Started

Step 1 - Launch Melon Pitcher and allow microphone access when prompted. The app needs microphone permission to detect the knock sound on a watermelon.
Step 2 - On the Home screen, select the watermelon variety you are testing (or leave it as General Watermelon).
Step 3 - Tap "Start Detection" and knock the watermelon firmly three times as guided on screen.
Tip: Knock in a quiet environment and hold your iPhone's microphone close (within a few inches) of the watermelon for the most accurate reading.

2. Reading the Result

  • Ripeness Level - Unripe, Raw-Ripe, Perfect Ripe, or Overripe, based on the detected pitch frequency.
  • Pitch Frequency (Hz) - The dominant knock frequency detected in the 200-350 Hz watermelon band.
  • Estimated Brix - An estimated sweetness value derived from the pitch frequency.
  • Waveform Comparison - Your knock's waveform plotted against a reference "perfect ripe" waveform.

3. Choosing a Watermelon Variety

Different watermelon varieties have slightly different rind density and knock acoustics. Selecting the correct variety on the Home screen applies a small frequency offset for a more accurate ripeness estimate. If you're not sure of the variety, "General Watermelon" works well for most cases.

4. Adjusting Detection Sensitivity

If the app isn't picking up your knocks, or is picking up too much background noise, go to Settings → Detection Sensitivity and choose:

  • Low - Requires firm knocking. Best for loud environments.
  • Normal - Recommended for most environments.
  • High - Picks up light knocks. Use in quiet environments.

5. Reviewing History

Every completed detection is saved to the History tab with its variety, ripeness, pitch frequency, and timestamp. Swipe left on any record to delete it, or use "Clear All Local Data" in Settings to remove everything at once.

6. Display Mode

Melon Pitcher follows your iOS system appearance by default. You can lock the app to Light or Dark mode at any time in Settings → Display Mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Melon Pitcher ask for microphone permission?

Microphone access is essential for Melon Pitcher's core functionality - capturing and analyzing the pitch of your knock on the watermelon. All audio is processed locally on your device. No audio data is uploaded or transmitted to any server. You can revoke this permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Does Melon Pitcher require an internet connection?

No. All core detection functionality works completely offline. An internet connection is only used if you choose to share the app or leave an App Store rating.

How accurate is the ripeness detection?

Melon Pitcher analyzes the dominant frequency of your knock using FFT-based pitch detection in the 200-350 Hz band associated with watermelon ripeness. Results are a helpful guide based on acoustic analysis, not a guaranteed measurement, and should be combined with your own judgment (e.g. checking the field spot and the dried tendril).

The app isn't detecting my knocks. What should I do?

Try moving your iPhone's microphone closer to the watermelon, knocking more firmly, reducing ambient noise, and increasing the Detection Sensitivity in Settings. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@163.com.

Does Melon Pitcher collect any personal data?

No. Melon Pitcher does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All audio processing happens locally on your device and raw audio is deleted within 2 seconds of analysis. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is Melon Pitcher a paid app or does it have in-app purchases?

Melon Pitcher is a one-time paid app with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.
