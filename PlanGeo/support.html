Support & Feedback — PlanGeo

PlanGeo is developed and maintained by the STEM Academic Tools Team. Here is how to get help, report problems and send ideas — and what to expect back.

Effective date: 23 September 2026

The short version. Email support@daibou007.com — we read everything and typically reply within 3 business days. The in-app manual answers most how-to questions.

1. Before writing in

Most answers live inside the app itself:

  • User Manual — Settings → User Manual (or the sidebar). Nine chapters covering construction, constraints, measurement, theorems, locus, cases, the problem solver, archiving/export and common errors.
  • Guided tours — Settings → Guides & Tutorials replays the welcome onboarding and the canvas walkthrough at any time.
  • Check for Updates — Settings → Check for Updates tells you whether your installed version is the latest release.

PlanGeo is fully offline: constructions, archives and preferences stay on your device, so a reinstall or device change means starting a fresh archive — there is no cloud copy for us (or anyone) to restore.

2. Contacting support

ChannelAddressBest for
Emailsupport@daibou007.comBug reports, geometry-correctness questions, feature ideas
App Store reviewVia the App Store pagePublic feedback and ratings

When reporting a problem, include:

  1. App version (Settings → Check for Updates shows it, e.g. 1.0.0 (1));
  2. iPad model and iPadOS version;
  3. What you did and what you expected vs. what happened;
  4. For wrong measurements or theorem results: the construction steps so we can rebuild the scene.

3. What happens next

  • We read every message. Typical first response is within 3 business days.
  • Bug fixes ship as App Store updates; the version note in the update listing describes what changed.
  • Feature requests are recorded against the syllabus roadmap (US high school geometry, honors geometry and AP Precalculus) and prioritized by how many learners they help.

4. Scope of support

  • We support the current App Store version and the one immediately before it.
  • We cannot recover deleted local archives (PlanGeo stores everything only on your device — see the Privacy Policy).
  • Support is provided in English.
