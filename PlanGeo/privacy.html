Privacy Policy — PlanGeo

PlanGeo is a professional offline 2D Euclidean plane geometry tool for iPad. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and it does not collect personal data.

Effective date: 23 September 2026

The short version. Everything PlanGeo constructs, solves and exports is computed on your device. Nothing about you or your geometry is transmitted to us, because there is nothing in the app that transmits it. The only network feature is a manual update check that you trigger.

1. Who we are

PlanGeo is the seventh product of the STEM academic tool suite, published by Daibou007 Team ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the PlanGeo application (com.daibou007.PlanGeo) and nothing else.

2. No tracking, no advertising, no analytics

We collect no personal information. Specifically, PlanGeo does not:

  • create or require an account, a sign-in, or a profile;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
  • collect location data of any kind;
  • collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics;
  • contain advertising, marketing pop-ups, or third-party tracking SDKs;
  • use cookies or any equivalent tracking technology;
  • read your photos, microphone, or camera.

3. Information stored on your device

PlanGeo saves your work locally so the app is useful between sessions. This data stays in the app's own storage on your device and is never uploaded, synced, or shared:

  • Saved constructions and records — the geometric scenes, theorem-verification schemes and locus records you explicitly save, written as JSON files inside the app container;
  • Preferences — theme (light/dark), canvas options such as grid and axis visibility, snapping, measurement precision and first-launch guidance flags, stored in the standard iOS UserDefaults.

Deleting the app — or using Settings → Clear all local data — permanently removes all of it. There is no cloud copy for us (or anyone) to restore.

4. The only network feature: manual update check

When you explicitly tap Settings → Check for Updates, PlanGeo queries Apple's public iTunes Lookup API (itunes.apple.com/lookup) with the app's bundle identifier to read the latest published version number. The check:

  • runs only when you tap — the app never initiates it in the background;
  • sends nothing about you beyond the bundle identifier Apple already publishes;
  • returns a version string that is compared on-device.

5. Apple rating prompt

When you choose Settings → Rate PlanGeo, the app calls Apple's standard SKStoreReviewController API. Apple controls that prompt and any data Apple's own system services collect. The prompt is strictly manual — it is never triggered automatically and never uses induced wording.

6. External links

The app's Settings and About pages can open this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service and the Support page in your web browser. Those requests are handled by your browser, not by the app.

7. Children

PlanGeo is designed for classroom and self-study use, including middle- and high-school students. Because the app collects nothing, it is safe for use by children under applicable privacy laws (including COPPA and GDPR-K): there is no personal information to collect.

8. Privacy manifest

The app ships an Apple privacy manifest declaring no tracking, no collected data types, and only the required accessed-API reasons (user defaults, file timestamps, system boot time) used for local app functionality.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this document will be updated and the app's version number incremented before that version ships.
