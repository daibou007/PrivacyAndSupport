Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Summary: Magic Pie is a fully offline, privacy-first spin-art drawing app. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your drawings, saved works, music imports and preferences stay entirely on your iPad.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Magic Pie ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our iPad application. Magic Pie is a turntable drawing studio: you paint on a rotating disc in layers, merge them into a single spinning artwork, and export it as a video or image. It works entirely on your device — there is no account, no server, and no tracking.

By using Magic Pie, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Magic Pie does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or track your usage behavior.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not upload your drawings, saved works, music files, or any content to any server.
  • We do not display advertising, and we do not share data with data brokers.

Magic Pie makes no network requests of its own. The only time the App opens a web page is when you deliberately tap a legal or App Store link in Settings or on the About page — that page then opens in your system browser, under your browser's own privacy policy.

3. Permissions and Device Access

Magic Pie asks for exactly one permission, and only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Photos (add only) — requested the first time you tap Save to Photos after an export, so the finished video or image can be written to your photo library. The App can only add items; it cannot read, browse, or modify your existing photos.

Magic Pie does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, health data, Bluetooth, or any other sensitive device resource.

Screen recording: MP4 exports are rendered frame by frame on your device with Apple's AVFoundation — the App does not record your screen for them. Separately, the fullscreen playback view offers an optional REC button, which uses Apple's ReplayKit to record the playing animation on screen and save it to your photo library. Recording runs only while you have it switched on, is written to a local file, is never sent anywhere, and does not capture audio from the microphone.

4. Data Storage

Everything you create is stored locally on your iPad:

  • Drafts and saved works — your layers, strokes, merged layers and disc settings are saved as local files in the App's own storage folder, protected by iOS file protection (encrypted at rest while your iPad is locked). Merging, reordering, hiding or rotating layers happens entirely on your device and changes only these local files.
  • Preferences — appearance, canvas color, brush width, rotation speed, onion opacity, BGM track, volume and export settings are kept in iOS system preferences on your device.
  • Imported music — see section 6 below.

None of this data is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the App removes all of it permanently; you can also clear everything from Settings → Cache → Clear All Local Data inside the App.

5. Your Artwork and Exports

You own everything you draw. When you export a video or image, Magic Pie renders the file on your device and then hands it to the standard iOS share sheet — you choose the destination (Photos, Files, AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or another app), and iOS performs the transfer. The App itself never sends your artwork anywhere.

6. Imported Music

Magic Pie ships with four built-in background-music loops and also lets you add your own audio files from the Files app. When you pick a file, the App copies it into its own private storage folder on your iPad so it can play offline; the original file is untouched and the copy never leaves your device. Imported music is deleted along with the App, or when you remove the track or clear local data inside the App.

7. Third-Party Services

Magic Pie does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social login services. The App is built entirely on Apple system frameworks (SwiftUI, UIKit, AVFoundation, ReplayKit, StoreKit and Photos), and no data is shared with any third party.

8. Children's Privacy

Magic Pie does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and is safe for all ages.

9. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. All your data lives on your device and can be removed simply by uninstalling the App or clearing local data in Settings.
