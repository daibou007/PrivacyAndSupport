Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Summary: Clash of Naval is a turn-based naval battle game. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. There is no sign-in of any kind (including Apple sign-in): an anonymous player profile is created only if you opt to make one in-game, and all game preferences are stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Clash of Naval ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. Clash of Naval is a Battleship-style tactical duel game for iPhone and iPad, playable offline against a bot opponent or online against another player.

By using Clash of Naval, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Clash of Naval does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contact details. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account with real identity details to play.
  • The App may generate a random anonymous player ID and display name (e.g. "Player7K2M") to identify you in online matches, leaderboards, and match history — but only if you opt in by tapping "Create Account" in Settings; without an account you play as an anonymous guest and no profile is stored for you. This ID is not linked to your real identity.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your camera, photo library, contacts, or location.

Gameplay preferences (sound, vibration, display settings, ship skin selection) are stored locally on your device.

3. Online Multiplayer & Network Access

Clash of Naval requires network access (Wi-Fi or cellular) to offer online multiplayer: creating/joining rooms, quick matchmaking, and exchanging turn-based game moves (ship placement, shots, results) with our game server over an encrypted WebSocket connection (wss://). iOS may prompt for local network access when the App looks for or connects to game servers on your network — this is used solely to establish gameplay connections, not to scan or profile devices on your network for any other purpose.

Data exchanged during an online match is limited to gameplay information (moves, room codes, match results, your anonymous player ID) and basic match statistics (wins, losses, shot accuracy) used to calculate a skill rating and recommend a fitting bot difficulty. No personal information is transmitted.

4. Camera, Photo Library & Near-Field Communication (NFC)

Clash of Naval does not request or use camera access, photo library access, or near-field communication (NFC). The App has no features that require these permissions, and none of them are used anywhere in the game.

5. Player Identity (No Account Required)

Clash of Naval never asks you to sign in with Apple or any other identity provider, and we never receive your Apple ID or email. There is no automatic registration: an account exists only if you choose to create one by tapping "Create Account" in Settings. When you do, the App generates a random account code on your device and stores it locally; the game server only ever sees a scrambled (hashed) form of this code as your anonymous player ID, so no identifier that can be linked back to you is transmitted or stored. Without an account you can still play offline and join online matches as a fully anonymous guest — no profile is stored for you at all. With an account you may pick a nickname once from Settings (optional, and not verified against any real identity), and you can permanently delete your profile at any time via Settings → Delete Account.

6. Player Reports & Blocking

To keep online matches fair, you may report or block another player from within an active match. A report transmits only the match's room identifier, the reported player's anonymous ID, and the report reason (e.g. offensive nickname) to our moderation system — no chat logs, images, or other personal data are collected as part of this feature (Clash of Naval has no free-text chat).

7. Feedback

The App offers an optional in-game feedback form (category and message, with an optional contact field if you choose to provide one). Feedback is submitted voluntarily and used only to improve the game. Do not include personal information you do not wish to share.

8. Data Storage

Local gameplay preferences and statistics are stored on your device and are removed automatically when you uninstall the App, or immediately via Settings → Delete Data. Online profile data (anonymous ID, display name, win/loss record, leaderboard rank) is stored on our servers only for as long as your account is active, and is permanently deleted via Settings → Delete Account.

9. Third-Party Services

Clash of Naval does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social login services. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

10. Children's Privacy

Clash of Naval does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App does not require real names, email addresses, or other identifying details to play.

11. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal information, there is little for us to access, correct, or delete beyond your anonymous game profile. You can delete your local data and/or your server-side account and profile at any time from within the App: Settings → Delete Data / Delete Account.

If you have questions or concerns, please contact us at daibou007@gmail.com.
