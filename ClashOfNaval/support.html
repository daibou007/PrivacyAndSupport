How to Play Clash of Naval

1. Starting a Match

Step 1 — From the Lobby, choose how to play: Local Battle against the built-in bot (offline, pick a difficulty), Campaign (PvE training ladder against a server-side AI), or Online Multiplayer (create a private room, join a room by code, or Quick Match).
Step 2 — Optionally adjust match settings: board size (Small 8×8 / Medium 10×10 / Large 15×15), whether Area Attack (a special aerial-bombing ammo type) is enabled, Blitz mode (10-second turn timer), and your Draft Fleet.
Step 3 — On the Deploy screen, tap to place each ship on the grid, rotate as needed, or tap Randomize to auto-place your fleet, then tap Ready.

2. Taking Your Turn

  1. Take turns tapping a cell on the opponent's grid to fire. A hit lets you keep shooting; a miss passes the turn to the opponent.
  2. A hit is marked with a flame icon; a miss is marked with a splash icon; sinking a ship reveals its full silhouette.
  3. Sink every ship in the enemy fleet before yours goes down to win the match.
  4. Hitting a Shield ends your turn immediately (see Advanced Operations below). In Blitz mode, running out of the 10-second timer auto-fires a random shot.
Tip: Offline matches against the bot work completely without an internet connection. Online multiplayer requires Wi-Fi or cellular data.

3. Advanced Operations (Skills)

Advanced skills share one charge pool. Sink enemy ships to earn charge points (100 points per charge), then use them wisely:

  • Bomb Row / Area Attack (Cost 1) — Strike an entire row at once.
  • Shield (Cost 1) — Protect one of your own not-yet-hit ship cells. The shield shatters on hit, absorbs the damage, and the attacker's turn ends — the cell stays intact and can be hit again.
  • Sonar Ping (Cost 1) — Scan a 3×3 area: red border means a ship is inside, blue means empty.
  • Decoy (Cost 1) — Plant a fake target on your board. A hit on it looks real to the enemy but sinks nothing.
  • Mine Lay (Cost 2) — Plant a mine on your board. A shot that lands on it becomes a guaranteed miss (white splash) and the attacker's next turn is skipped once.
  • Repair (Cost 2) — Heal one of your own damaged (not sunk) cells back to full health.

4. Spectating a Private Match

Private rooms can be shared with a friend using the room's 4-character code so they can watch the match live. Spectating is only available for private rooms, not Quick Match games.

5. Skill Rating & Bot Difficulty

Clash of Naval tracks your win rate, shot accuracy, and recent match results to estimate a skill rating and recommend a fitting bot difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard). You can also manually override the bot difficulty from the Lobby. Seasons reset every 28 days with ranks from Recruit to Admiral.

6. Reporting an Opponent

If an online opponent is behaving unfairly, use the in-match Report button to flag them to our moderation system, or block them from the Settings/Profile screen to avoid future matches with them.

7. Customizing Settings

  • Sound & Vibration — Toggle sound effects, music, and haptic feedback
  • Display — Adjust display-related preferences
  • Ship Skin — Choose a visual fleet skin
  • Account — View your player profile, sign in, or sign out
  • Delete Data / Delete Account — Clear local preferences, or permanently delete your online profile and server-side data

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to play Clash of Naval?

No, not for offline play. You can play a full match against the built-in bot AI with no internet connection. Online multiplayer (creating/joining rooms, Quick Match, leaderboards) requires an internet connection.

Why does Clash of Naval ask for local network access?

The App may prompt for local network access when connecting to our online multiplayer servers. This permission is used solely to establish gameplay connections — Clash of Naval does not use your camera, photo library, or near-field communication (NFC), and does not scan your local network for any other purpose.

Does Clash of Naval require an account?

No. You can play offline immediately. Online multiplayer identifies you with a randomly generated anonymous player ID and display name — no real name, email, or personal information is required.

Is my data backed up to iCloud?

Local gameplay preferences are stored only on your device. Your online profile (anonymous ID, win/loss record, leaderboard rank) is stored on our game servers for as long as your account is active. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Delete Data to clear local preferences, or Settings → Delete Account to permanently delete your online profile and all associated server-side data.

The app crashed or isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem, your device model, and iOS version.

Does Clash of Naval collect any personal data?

No. Clash of Naval does not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
