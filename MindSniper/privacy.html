Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: MindSniper is a mind mapping app for iPhone and iPad. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. There is no account, no sign-in of any kind (including Apple sign-in), and no network connection is ever made by the App. Your mind maps and notes are stored only on your own device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MindSniper ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. MindSniper is a gesture-based mind mapping tool: you create idea nodes, link them with connection lines, cut unwanted connections with a simple swipe, rearrange ideas freely, and export your maps as images or documents.

By using MindSniper, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

MindSniper does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contact details. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account, and there is no sign-in of any kind (including Apple sign-in).
  • We do not use advertising SDKs and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your camera, photo library, contacts, location, microphone, or near-field communication (NFC).
  • The App does not contact any server for any reason. It works fully offline.

3. Your Mind Maps Are Stored Only on Your Device

All content you create — nodes, text, connections, and map layout — is stored in local files inside the App's private sandbox on your device. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or synced to any server, and we cannot see or access your maps.

To help you recover from mistakes, the App keeps automatic version snapshots of each map (the most recent 10 snapshots per map) on your device. App preferences (such as operation mode, colors, and appearance) are stored in your device's standard local preference storage.

4. Sharing and Exporting

MindSniper can export a map as PNG, PDF, SVG, JSON, OPML, Markdown, or plain text. Exporting always uses the iOS share sheet: you choose the destination (e.g. Files, Mail, AirDrop) and nothing leaves your device except through an action you explicitly take. Exported files are created in the App's temporary storage and are removed automatically.

5. Network Access

MindSniper makes no network connections. The App has no servers, no online features, and no third-party analytics, advertising, or social login services. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

MindSniper does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App does not require real names, email addresses, or other identifying details to use any feature.

7. Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

Because your data never leaves your device, you are always in full control of it. You can delete a map at any time from the map list in the App, and all data is removed when you uninstall the App. If you have questions or concerns, please contact us at daibou007@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
