How to Use MindSniper

1. Getting Started

Step 1 — On the map list, tap + to create a new map, or open one of the sample maps that are created for you on first launch.
Step 2 — Tap the + button on the toolbar or FAB to add an idea node, then double-tap a node to edit its text, description, color, emoji, and shape.
Step 3 — Drag nodes anywhere on the canvas. Pinch to zoom in and out, and drag with one finger to pan around large maps.

2. Connecting and Cutting Lines

  1. Connect — Select a node, then drag from it to another node to draw a connection line between your ideas.
  2. Cut — Drag your finger or Apple Pencil straight across a connection line to cut it. The cut line snaps apart — that's the signature MindSniper move for pruning ideas.
  3. Rearrange — Drag an unselected node to move it; drag a selected node to draw a new connection from it.
Tip: Two operation modes are available in Settings → Interaction & Style. Single-Hand (Apple Pencil): tap to select, drag a selected node to connect, drag empty canvas to cut lines. Two-Hand (Modifier): hold the floating Hold button while dragging to connect, or pan without it to move.

3. Styling Your Map

  • Nodes — Change node color, emoji, shape, font size/boldness, text alignment, flag a node, or set task progress.
  • Connection lines — Choose curve, straight, or dashed styles; add arrowheads (V line, solid, hollow, diamond, dot) on one or both ends; pick a connection color.
  • Canvas — Switch backgrounds (plain, dark, paper ruled, graph paper, dotted, and more) and toggle the grid. Dark Mode is available in Settings.

4. Automatic Layouts

Tap any layout button in the toolbar to instantly rearrange your whole map:

  • Organic — a force-directed layout that untangles knots naturally.
  • Radial Ring — a circular arrangement around a central idea.
  • Mind Map Tree — the classic balanced left-right tree layout.

5. History, Undo, and Versions

  • Undo / Redo — undo or redo your last edit at any time from the toolbar.
  • Version snapshots — the App automatically keeps the most recent 10 snapshots of each map on your device so you can roll back after big changes.
  • Map list — rename, duplicate, or delete maps from the map list in the sidebar (iPad) or on the home screen (iPhone).

6. Exporting and Sharing

Export any map from the toolbar: PNG image, PDF, SVG vector, JSON data, OPML (for other mind map apps), Markdown task checklist, or plain text. The iOS share sheet lets you save to Files, AirDrop, Mail, and more. Exports never leave your device unless you choose where to send them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does MindSniper need an internet connection?

No. MindSniper is fully offline — it never connects to any server for any reason.

Where is my data stored?

Your maps and notes are stored only in local files in the App's private area on your device, along with automatic version snapshots and your display preferences. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does MindSniper sync to iCloud or any cloud service?

No. MindSniper does not sync your maps to iCloud or any other cloud service. If you want a copy elsewhere, use Export and save the file wherever you like.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Delete a map from the map list at any time, or uninstall the App to remove everything at once.

Why can't I cut a connection line?

Make sure the line isn't selected first, then swipe cleanly across it. Cutting needs a single straight drag across the line with a small pause at the end — practice once and it becomes second nature.

The app crashed or isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem, your device model, and iOS version.

Does MindSniper collect any personal data?

No. MindSniper does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
