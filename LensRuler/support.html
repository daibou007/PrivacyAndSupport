How to Use LensRuler

1. Getting Started with AR

Step 1 — Open the app and point your iPhone camera at a flat surface (floor, table, or wall). Wait for the surface detection grid to appear.
Step 2 — Move your phone slowly to help ARKit map the environment. The grid overlay confirms a surface has been detected.
Step 3 — Once the surface is detected, tap to place measurement points.
Tip: Measurement accuracy is highest in well-lit environments. Avoid measuring on reflective or transparent surfaces like glass or mirrors.

2. Point-to-Point Measurement

Measure the distance between any two points in real space:

  1. Tap the screen to place your first measurement point on a detected surface.
  2. Move your phone to the endpoint and tap again to place the second point.
  3. The distance is displayed instantly in your preferred unit (ft/in or cm/m).
  4. Tap Save to capture an annotated photo of the measurement.

3. Area Measurement

Calculate the area of any space:

  1. Switch to Area mode from the measurement toolbar.
  2. Tap three or more points around the perimeter of the area.
  3. The app automatically calculates and displays the enclosed area in real time.
  4. Tap Save to capture an annotated photo.
Tip: For best accuracy with area measurement, place all points on the same flat surface.

4. Height Measurement

Measure wall heights, ceiling heights, and vertical dimensions:

  1. Switch to Height mode from the measurement toolbar.
  2. Point your camera at the base of what you want to measure and tap to set the start point.
  3. Tilt the phone upward and tap to set the top point.
  4. The vertical height is displayed with the measurement overlay.

5. Saving Annotated Photos

After any measurement, tap Save Photo to capture the AR measurement overlay as an annotated image saved directly to your Camera Roll. The photo includes all measurement labels and lines drawn on the live camera view.

Tip: You need to grant photo library access the first time you save a photo. You can manage this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

6. Viewing Measurement History

All your saved measurements are stored in the History tab:

  1. Tap the History tab at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Browse your saved measurements sorted by date.
  3. Tap any entry to view the annotated photo and measurement details.
  4. Swipe left on any entry to delete it.

7. Switching Units

Toggle between metric (cm/m) and imperial (ft/in) units at any time:

  • Tap the unit toggle on the measurement screen, or
  • Go to Settings → Measurement → Unit System

Your preferred unit is saved automatically.

8. Customizing Settings

  • Appearance — Choose Dark Mode, Light Mode, or Auto (follows system)
  • Unit System — Switch between metric (cm/m) and imperial (ft/in)
  • Precision — Set decimal precision to 0.1 or 0.01
  • Haptics — Enable or disable haptic feedback on point placement

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does LensRuler ask for camera permission?

Camera access is required for augmented reality measurement. LensRuler uses ARKit to detect real-world surfaces and calculate distances through your camera. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device in real-time and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. You can revoke this permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Why does LensRuler ask for photo library permission?

Photo library (add-only) access is used to save annotated measurement photos to your Camera Roll. The app can only add photos — it cannot read, modify, or delete your existing photos. This permission is only requested when you tap "Save Photo." You can revoke it anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Does LensRuler work on all iPhones?

LensRuler works on any iPhone running iOS 17.6 or later. Devices with a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later) will achieve higher accuracy. On non-LiDAR devices, ARKit world tracking is used, which works well in most conditions.

Why isn't the surface being detected?

Surface detection works best in well-lit environments. Try the following: ensure the area is well-lit, move your camera slowly across the surface, and avoid highly reflective or transparent surfaces (glass, mirrors, shiny floors). The detection grid appears on screen when a surface is recognized.

How accurate are the measurements?

On LiDAR-enabled devices (iPhone 12 Pro or later), LensRuler achieves accuracy within 1–2 cm over typical home distances (1–5 m). On non-LiDAR devices, accuracy varies based on lighting and surface texture. An accuracy indicator is shown on screen to guide you.

Does LensRuler work offline?

Yes! LensRuler works completely offline. All AR processing, measurement, and photo saving features work without an internet connection. No internet is ever required.

Where is my data stored?

All measurement history, saved photos, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to external servers. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Does LensRuler collect any personal data?

No. LensRuler does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All processing happens locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The AR isn't tracking well. What should I do?

Try these steps: ensure good lighting, move your camera slowly to let ARKit build its map, reset the AR session by going back to the home screen and re-entering measurement mode. If problems persist, force-quit and relaunch the app.

The app crashed or isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If issues persist, please email us at daibou007@gmail.com with details about the problem and your device model & iOS version.
